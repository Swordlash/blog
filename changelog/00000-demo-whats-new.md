## New features

The demo now has the page "What's new?", as a server does: the link at the
bottom of the sidebar opens the newest changes, and a dot on it marks those
you have not seen. Open it from the demo's sidebar, or at its address
under the demo's own:

```text
<the demo's address>/whats-new/
```
