## Bug fixes

- In the editor, "Draft saved" used to appear beside Save, Save and close
  and Cancel, and pushed them along as it appeared: a quick click meant for
  Save could land on Cancel. It now shows as a small grey notice under the
  editor's top bar, at its right, and the buttons stay where they are. The
  green "Saved" shown after a save now appears in the same place, rather
  than in the bottom right corner of the window. On a phone both show in
  the bottom right corner, above the help button.
