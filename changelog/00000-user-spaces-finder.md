## New features

"User subspaces" in the sidebar is no longer greyed out once there is a
personal space you may open: yours, or another user's public one. It
shows a person (👤) for its icon, and while your personal space is on,
"Your personal space" sits right under it, one click away.

A click on "User subspaces", in the sidebar or on the front page, opens
the finder of user spaces at its own address:

```text
<the wiki's address>/user-spaces/
```

At the top, under **Starred**, are your own space and every user space
whose home page you starred: a star on a space's home page stars the
space. Under **Find another**, type a few letters of someone's login or
name, such as `rita`, and their space is listed below.
