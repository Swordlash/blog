## Other changes

- Every space now has a home page, the page it opens at. A new space gets
  one titled "Home", as before, and keeps it: the space's settings no longer
  choose another page or none, and a page no longer has "Set as home page".
- A space that had no home page gets one when the server is upgraded, with
  the text a new space starts with. A space whose settings said "None" may
  still hold the page it was made with, at an address ending in `home`; the
  new home page then goes beside it, at one ending in `home-2`.
- The home page cannot be deleted or moved, and neither can a page with the
  home page under it be deleted or moved to another space. It can still be
  moved up or down among the pages beside it.
- Only whoever administers a space edits its home page, renames it,
  restores an older version of it and changes its attachments. Everyone
  who edits the space still adds pages under it and comments on it.
- A blog shows its home page above its posts only once someone has written
  it: a home page that still has the text it was made with is left out.
- The container "User subspaces" and an encrypted space still have no home
  page, as before.
