## Bug fixes

- The header's menus no longer stay open when you click somewhere else.
  The Starred list, the notifications under the bell and the demo's menu of
  users now close when you click anywhere outside them, or press Escape,
  as the search box's list already did. So do the sidebar's "Switch space"
  menu and the editor's Box, HTML and Colour menus: clicking in the text
  closes an open toolbar menu. Clicking a menu's own button still closes
  it, and opening one menu closes any other.
